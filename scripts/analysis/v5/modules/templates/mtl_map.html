Two partitions, two labels — read together. The grid axis bounds the distance: the prediction is right when it falls in the shaded TG grid, and 1 / 2 / N grids out grades how far. The cell axis gives the direction: whether it landed in the TG's own serving region or a neighbour's. The cells are unbounded. Every point on Earth is nearest to some seed, so cell_label alone will call a prediction correct at any distance — the grid axis is what bounds it. The dotted rectangle is a rendering bound, not a cell edge: every cell would continue past it. A seed is the spherical centroid of the sites grouped by complete linkage, not a grid centre.
